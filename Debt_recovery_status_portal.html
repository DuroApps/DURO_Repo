Reminder letter templates (Word .docx)
Upload up to four Word templates. They are embedded in the dashboard and reused by the Reminder Letter Management tab. To auto-fill customer details, put merge fields in your Word file using curly braces — supported tags: {date} {customer_code} {customer_name} {branch} {amount} {address} {city} {state} {zip} {email}. A template with no tags is still generated as a per-customer copy.
